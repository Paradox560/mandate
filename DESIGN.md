# Mandate interface

The workspace is an operating tool for small investment teams. Use a calm, light portfolio blotter: graphite navigation rail, ivory working surface, sharp tables, charcoal text, orange primary action, semantic forest green and muted red. No decorative finance imagery. Dense but readable, 14px body and tabular monospace measurements. A large functional equity chart anchors the overview; research lives beside the market and its citations open an evidence drawer. Execution permission checks are a legible checklist, never hidden behind a score.

Primary flow: watchlist symbol → cited research → proposed order → checks → approval → paper execution → audit. Research, Orders, Mandate, Audit trail and Connections are real navigation surfaces. Synthetic quotes and historical source dates remain visible. Never invent live integrations, performance or sources.
